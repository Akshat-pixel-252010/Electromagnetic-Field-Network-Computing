# Fields

Status: Under Research

## Fundamental Question

What is a field?

## Working Hypothesis (Version 1)

A field is a physical quantity that has a value at every point in space and time.

A field is not an object.

A field is a description of how physical influence is distributed throughout space.

Examples:

* Electric field
* Magnetic field
* Electromagnetic field
* Gravitational field

## Important Observation

Fields can:

* Store energy
* Carry information
* Interact with matter
* Interact with other fields
* Propagate disturbances

These properties make fields potential computational media.

## Electromagnetic Fields

Electromagnetic fields are currently the primary candidate for EFNC.

Reasons:

* Well understood physics
* Easy generation and detection
* Can propagate through space
* Support interference
* Support resonance
* Can carry phase and amplitude information
