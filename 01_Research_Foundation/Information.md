# Information

Status: Under Research

## Fundamental Question

What is information from the perspective of physics?

## Working Hypothesis (Version 1)

Information is not matter.

Information is not energy.

Information is a distinguishable physical state.

If two physical states can be distinguished, they can represent information.

Examples:

* Voltage high / voltage low
* Light on / light off
* Spin up / spin down
* Different electromagnetic wave phases
* Different electromagnetic wave amplitudes

## Consequence

Information requires a physical medium.

The medium may be:

* Electrons
* Photons
* Electromagnetic fields
* Magnetic domains
* Mechanical vibrations
* Biological neurons

The medium changes.

The information concept remains.

## Open Questions

1. Can information exist without a physical medium?
2. Is information fundamental to the universe?
3. Why can physical states represent information?
4. What is the relationship between information and entropy?
5. Is computation simply the transformation of information?
